# M-ID v36-21 · living card frames (jade dragon, peeking kitty…) + avatar decorations on every card style
- **Studio → Look → Card frame** (new): Jade dragon, Peeking kitty, Tech panel, Jade garden, Neon edge, or None. Works on **Basic, Glass slides and Star** cards. Live in the Studio preview, saved with Publish.
- **Jade dragon:** drawn from scratch and animated live. Its body coils around the card, weaving behind the edge and out again, with a cream mane and gold scales. The head breathes embers, blinks, opens its jaw and sways its whiskers, and a claw grips the card edge.
- **Peeking kitty:** fluffy trim, ears that twitch at different times, a face that blinks, paws that tap, and a tail that swishes.
- **Avatar decoration** (new, same Look tab): Neon ring, Holo spin, Dotted orbit, Sparkles, Cat ears, Halo, Leaf wreath, Glitch, with a colour. Shown on the Basic, Glass and Star avatars.
- Frames are a click-through layer drawn on top of the page, so they never change your card layout and are never clipped. They follow the card while scrolling, hide when the card isn't on screen, and stop moving for visitors with "reduce motion".
- No database change. Profile fields `frame`, `avatarDeco`, `avatarDecoColor` are saved with the rest of the profile.
- Not included: the Free canvas has its own frames (v36-20) but not the dragon/kitty yet.
