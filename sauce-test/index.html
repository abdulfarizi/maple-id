The story

Made slow.
Eaten fast.

Ember & Co. started in a rented kitchen with one stubborn idea: heat should taste like something, not just hurt.

01

Roast it first

Peppers and garlic go over open flame until they char, then sit for a day. That char is where the depth comes from.

02

Simmer for hours

Each batch simmers low and slow in a single pot, stirred by hand. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is added to hide a flavour.

03

Bottle with heat

Every jar is sealed while still warm and sent out the same week it is made. Open one and you will taste the difference.
