Guides

New here? Start with these

Register

How to register

  1. Tap Claim my ID above, or open Join M-ID.
  2. Type your name in the link box. It is checked live, so you will see if it is free.
  3. Sign up with your email to lock the link to your account.
  4. Confirm the sign-up from your email if asked, then sign in.

Your link is yours after sign-up, and nobody else can take it.

Make your card

How to make your card

  1. Open Studio from the header once you are signed in.
  2. Add your details: game IDs, socials, online hours, music and anime.
  3. Pick a look in the Look tab: a theme, a banner and a card frame.
  4. Publish to save. Share your link, QR code or contact card anywhere.

You can change everything later in Studio.
